💖 To My Dearest Sister, Leena 💖

Hi akka,

I want us to celebrate Raksha Bandhan in this beautiful way every year, and I hope the bond we share remains just as special forever. No matter how many misunderstandings, fights, or moments of anger have come between us, I hope we can leave them behind, forgive one another, and continue being happy together.

You have been an important part of my life since I was little. If I have ever irritated you, hurt you, spoken harshly, or caused you anger in any way, I am truly sorry, akka. I may not always express myself perfectly, but I genuinely wish you happiness, peace, good health, and success in everything you do.

Wherever you are and whatever life brings your way, I will always wish the best for you.

If I do not call or message you sometimes, please do not think that I am ignoring you or that I am upset with you. I may simply be busy or caught up with something, but it does not mean that I have forgotten you or that anything is wrong between us.

To me, Raksha Bandhan is not limited to a single day or a thread tied around the wrist. Whenever you tie a rakhi, it becomes a symbol of our bond, trust, and responsibility toward one another. It reminds me of the promise that I will always stand by you, support you, protect you, and be there whenever you need me—no matter where I am or how much time has passed.

"A rakhi is not just a thread; it is a promise that distance, time, and misunderstandings can never break a brother-sister bond."
"Sisters may argue, scold, and annoy their brothers, but they will always have a special place in their family."
"The beauty of Raksha Bandhan is not in the rakhi itself, but in the memories and promises connected to it."

Akka, our relationship may not always be perfect, but it is very precious to me. We may disagree, get angry, or stop talking for a while, but you will always be my sister, and I will always be your brother. I hope this Raksha Bandhan brings us closer, fills our hearts with happiness, and gives us many more beautiful memories to cherish together.

Happy Raksha Bandhan, akka. I will always respect you, support you, and stand by you. 🪢✨
